# Preregistration v3 — 2026-10-05, before new feature computation

**Status:** falsifiable research hypotheses, not discovered faults or vents. The ranking is a qualitative prior on expected DTI opportunity and implementation cost, not a numeric forecast. The top candidate was selected on physical rationale before computing its MINE value. MINE will be reported as marginal dependence on the public catalogue mask; the hidden expert labels are unavailable, and zero in the public raster means *unlabelled*, not confirmed absence.

## Evidence and novelty scope

The official problem describes incomplete public fault labels and expert-identified faults outside the public USGS catalogue. Its target is fault traces, not geothermal vents directly. The task's 100 m products are a spatially coarse proxy for structures; they cannot establish a fault, fluid flow, temperature, or geothermal productivity at a pixel. The official USGS GeoDAWN release says its airborne magnetics/radiometrics support mapping of subsurface structure/geology and surface geology/soil composition; this supports testing geophysical contrasts, but does not establish that a particular contrast is diagnostic of a fault.

We searched the 36 pinned sibling-site snapshots plus this repository's code and feature registers. The proposals below are new feature definitions in GEMSDOE38, not claims of worldwide novelty. Related work exists in sibling repositories: multi-physics edge coincidence / intersection density, topographic one-sided scarp asymmetry, gravity/basement steps, magnetic texture, DEM drainage and radiometric features. Those overlaps are called out rather than hidden. In particular, the previous version-2 D result is not treated as a clean current-best holdout: its candidate was promoted after screening the same catalogue labels on the same four quadrants, and the final TIF changed emission density, spacing and catalogue post-processing. No weekly slot has been used.

## Locked analysis before feature implementation

1. Build J, K and L from the checksum-pinned 19-band mirror only; no fault labels, fault-distance rasters, previous predictions, scores, or hidden labels enter feature construction. Record intermediate feature hashes and finite/range diagnostics.
2. Estimate MINE `I(feature; public catalogue mask)` on all **5,167,373** footprint cells, with the observed natural prevalence, three fixed seeds, all-pixel evaluation and four spatial out-of-fold estimates. Keep negative DV estimates; report nats, seed variability, shuffled-label controls, and the estimator's finite-capacity/selection limitations. MINE ranks are descriptive because catalogue zero is unlabelled and MINE is marginal, not `I(Y; feature | existing features)`.
3. J is the predeclared primary, so the MINE result will not be used to swap in whichever candidate looks best on these same labels. Test fixed HGB settings on the four spatial quadrants with a 1 km training exclusion, equal 0.8% per-fold emission budget and 2.8-cell separation. Compare `19-band baseline`, the previous `19 + D` internal benchmark, and `19 + D + J`; use paired 20 km spatial-block bootstrap and show every fold. No threshold, density, spacing, buffer, or model tuning after seeing holdout results.
4. This test is a **catalogue-proxy** test, not a hidden-label test. The earlier H33 public score has no organizer-published file hash; the sibling H33 `live-mirror` receipt reports only 2 of 6 historical orderings reproduced (3 of 6 after its own calibration), and its `h33_holdout` receipt relies on an estimated hidden prevalence plus public-catalogue/SGMC proxies. It is useful context, but not a compatible independently reproducible current-best checkpoint. A positive J-vs-D result will therefore not authorize a competition slot; a comparable current-best checkpoint and hidden-target evidence remain unavailable.
5. Packaging is separate from validation. If the fixed J arm does not beat D under the paired gate, no J upload is recommended. Any download offered remains a research artifact, not a leaderboard claim; never upload automatically. Preserve the existing unique TIFF and its exact receipt until a replacement is both independently reproducible and better under the preregistered comparison.

## Post-analysis implementation review — amendment recorded separately

A final code audit found that the implemented L array did not match the locked signed opposing-side formula above. The existing L-named MINE values are therefore exploratory for an unsigned as-coded variant, not evidence for this registered hypothesis. The exact mismatch, original hashes and numerical record are preserved in [the v3 review amendment](hypotheses-v3-review-amendment.md). J and K definitions are unaffected; the primary J comparison and closed slot decision remain unchanged. This post-analysis note does not retroactively alter the preregistration.

## Reproducibility / amendment policy

Record source-data checksums, exact parameter values, MINE seeds and full-fold results in a dated JSON receipt. If a source field, split, transform, seed, or budget must change, do not overwrite this protocol: add a versioned amendment before rerunning. Keep historical failed results and uncertainty intact.
